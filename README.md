# 🤖 InterviewAI

### AI-Powered Interview Simulator

**Practice interviews. Improve your answers. Build confidence.**

InterviewAI is an **AI-powered interview simulation platform** designed to help students, freshers, and job seekers practice interviews in a realistic environment.

Instead of preparing from a list of static interview questions, InterviewAI acts like an **AI interviewer** that asks questions, listens to your answers, evaluates your performance, and provides personalized feedback.

---

## 🎯 Why InterviewAI?

Interviews can be stressful, especially when you don't have someone available to practice with.

Common problems faced by students and job seekers include:

* ❌ Not knowing what questions to practice
* ❌ Practicing only with predefined questions
* ❌ Not getting feedback on their answers
* ❌ Lack of realistic interview experience
* ❌ Difficulty identifying strengths and weaknesses

**InterviewAI aims to solve these problems by providing an interactive AI-powered interview experience.**

---

## 🚀 What Can You Do With InterviewAI?

### 🎯 Create a Personalized Interview

Choose the interview settings according to your target job:

**Job Role → Experience → Interview Type → Difficulty → Number of Questions**

For example:

```text
Role: Python Developer
Experience: Fresher
Type: Technical
Difficulty: Medium
Questions: 5
```

---

### 🤖 AI Interviewer

InterviewAI dynamically generates questions based on your selected interview configuration.

The AI can adapt questions to different roles and difficulty levels instead of simply showing a fixed list of questions.

It also keeps track of previously asked questions to help avoid repetition during the interview.

---

### 🎤 Answer Using Your Voice

InterviewAI supports **voice-based answers** using the browser's speech recognition capabilities.

You can answer naturally instead of typing every response.

```text
🎤 Speak your answer
        ↓
AI processes your response
        ↓
Answer is evaluated
```

---

### 🧠 AI-Powered Answer Evaluation

After answering a question, the AI evaluates your response based on multiple factors:

| Evaluation      | What it measures                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 🎯 Accuracy     | How correct your answer is          |
| 🔗 Relevance    | How closely it answers the question |
| 💬 Clarity      | How clearly you explain your idea   |
| 📝 Completeness | How thoroughly you answer           |

You don't just receive a score — you also get feedback that helps you understand **how to improve**.

---

### 📊 Performance Dashboard

After completing the interview, InterviewAI generates a performance summary.

It provides:

* ⭐ Overall Score
* 🎯 Accuracy Score
* 🔗 Relevance Score
* 💬 Clarity Score
* 📝 Completeness Score
* 💪 Strengths
* 📈 Areas for Improvement
* 📋 Question-by-Question Feedback

This allows you to understand your interview performance instead of simply knowing whether an answer was right or wrong.
